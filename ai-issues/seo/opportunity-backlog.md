## Demand and duplication review to perform before scheduling

For each candidate, record its actual query/page pairs where accessible, user feedback, overlapping canonical entries and the practical operation it adds. Ask whether a user would value it without search engines. The profile solves attribution; mobile help improves an existing failing journey; output inspection adds an original worked example only once the example exists. Sprite/DXF ideas do not yet pass complete-workflow or evidence gates.

Do not change established converter titles, URLs, defaults, canonical strategy or descriptions merely because a draft exists. Do not promise machine compatibility, original-quality photo reconstruction, universal offline processing or physical iPhone validation. No new authorship credentials, reviews, ratings, fresh dates, or platform endorsements are implied.

## Metrics after a real release

Record actual deployment/rollback SHA and observation dates; compare baseline and 7/28-day impressions, CTR, query position, relevant country/device sessions, successful conversion/downloads and available revenue without sending artwork or filenames. Separate seasonality and other technical releases. If unavailable, explicitly retain “unavailable.” Publication cadence is risk control, not a search-ranking guarantee.
